About Us

Our Story of Educational Excellence

NextGen Scholars is an academic coaching institute built on a simple belief — quality education with responsibility. We focus on concept clarity, discipline, and continuous improvement. Our approach is student-centric, ensuring that each learner gets the attention they deserve.

Our Mission

Nurturing Excellence, Building Futures

To build confident learners with strong academic foundations and the right mindset for future challenges. Our mission is to provide quality education in a supportive environment, to guide students with care, discipline, and responsibility, and to help students grow academically and personally.

"Education is not the filling of a pail, but the lighting of a fire. We ignite the passion for learning in every student."

— Dr. Rajesh Kumar, Founder

Founder

Mr. Nirad Baran Dalai

Mr. Nirad Baran Dalai believes that true education shapes character and transforms lives. With a background in Commerce (Finance) from KIIT University and BJB Autonomous College, along with a B.Ed. in Political Science and Social Studies, he brings strong conceptual clarity and learner-centric teaching to education.

With 6+ years of teaching experience across multiple boards and experience as a Guest Faculty in English and Communication, his focus remains on concept-based learning, discipline, and student confidence. His appearance in the UPSC Examination (2024) reflects his commitment to leadership and continuous growth.
